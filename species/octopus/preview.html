How to watch

Examine. A small turn comes before the reach. Neighbouring arms unfold toward the invitation while supporting arms keep their place. Release lets the tips peel back.

Startle. The arms gather, release their contacts, and stream behind a mantle-first jet. The return is slower; the arms find their footing at different moments.

Keyboard. Focus the artwork with Tab. Arrow keys move the invitation; Enter examines, J startles, Space pauses or plays, and Escape releases. All actions also have ordinary buttons. Tab always leaves the artwork.

Original procedural artwork, inspired by octopus anatomy. Motion timing, visible skin waves and the pale ink treatment are artistic interpretations.
