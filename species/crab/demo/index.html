Keyboard & touch field notes

Focus the artwork with Tab. Arrow keys move your guide; 1–5 select a resident. F invites foraging, S signals, H seeks shelter, N advances the chapter, Space pauses, and Escape clears the guide. All actions also have labelled controls. On touch, tap the sand, a resident or its rock; you can still scroll the page.
